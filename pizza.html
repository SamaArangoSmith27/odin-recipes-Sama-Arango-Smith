Pizza

Go back.

A chewy, golden crust topped with bright tomato sauce, melted mozzarella and fresh basil, baked hot and fast for crisp edges. Proof that the best pizza night starts with your own dough.

Ingredients

  • grams bread flour (or all-purpose)
  • milliliters warm water
  • grams instant yeast
  • grams salt
  • teaspoons sugar
  • tablespoons olive oil
  • grams canned crushed tomatoes
  • garlic clove, grated
  • teaspoons dried oregano
  • teaspoons salt, for the sauce
  • grams mozzarella, shredded
  • grams parmesan, grated
  • fresh basil leaves

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, stir together 500 grams bread flour (or all-purpose), 7 grams instant yeast, 1 teaspoons sugar and 10 grams salt. Add 325 milliliters warm water and 2 tablespoons olive oil and mix until a shaggy dough forms.
  2. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured counter and knead until smooth and springy. It should bounce back slowly when poked.
  3. Place the dough in an oiled bowl, cover, and leave somewhere warm until roughly doubled in size.
  4. While the dough rises, stir together 400 grams canned crushed tomatoes, 1 garlic clove, grated, 1 teaspoons dried oregano and 0.5 teaspoons salt, for the sauce. No cooking needed; the oven does that.
  5. Preheat the oven: Put a pizza stone, steel or upside-down baking sheet on the lowest rack and heat the oven as hot as it goes, 250-260C / 475-500F, for at least 30 minutes
  6. Punch the dough down, split it in two, and shape each into a ball. Cover and let relax so it stretches without springing back.
  7. On a floured surface or parchment, press and stretch one ball into a 12-inch round with your fingertips, leaving the edge slightly thicker. Avoid a rolling pin, which squeezes out the air.
  8. Spread half the sauce thinly, leaving a 2 cm border. Scatter half the 300 grams mozzarella, shredded and half the 40 grams parmesan, grated.
  9. Slide the pizza (on its parchment is fine) onto the hot stone or sheet. Bake until the crust is deep golden and the cheese is bubbling with brown spots. Repeat with the second pizza.
  10. Tear 10 fresh basil leaves over the top, let it sit a couple of minutes so the cheese sets, then slice.
