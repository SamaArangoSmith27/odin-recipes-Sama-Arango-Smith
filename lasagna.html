Lasagna

Go back

Layers of tender pasta, rich slow-simmered meat sauce, creamy ricotta and golden bubbling mozzarella, baked until the edges crisp. A comforting classic that feeds a crowd and tastes even better the next day.

Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 700 grams ground beef
  • 800 grams crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1.5 teaspoons salt
  • 0.5 teaspoons black pepper
  • 450 grams ricotta
  • 1 large egg
  • 450 grams mozzarella, shredded
  • 100 grams Parmesan, grated
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 0.3 cups fresh basil, chopped

Instructions:

  1. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add 1 medium onion, finely chopped and cook until soft and translucent, then add 4 garlic cloves, minced and cook one minute more until fragrant.
  2. Add 700 grams ground beef and break it up with a spoon. Cook until no pink remains and the bottom of the pot starts to brown. Drain off excess fat if there's more than a tablespoon or two.
  3. Stir in 800 grams crushed tomatoes, 2 tablespoons tomato paste, 1 teaspoons dried oregano, 1.5 teaspoons salt and 0.5 teaspoons black pepper. Bring to a gentle bubble, then lower the heat and simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, until thickened. Taste and adjust salt.
  4. In a bowl, combine 450 grams ricotta, 1 large egg, 0.3 cups fresh basil, chopped and half of 100 grams Parmesan, grated. Stir until smooth. Season with a pinch of salt.
  5. Heat the oven to 190C / 375F. Cook 12 lasagna noodles in well-salted boiling water until just shy of al dente, then lay them flat on a lightly oiled tray so they don't stick together.
  6. Spread a thin layer of sauce in a 9x13 pan. Add noodles, a third of the remaining sauce, a third of the ricotta mixture, and a handful of 450 grams mozzarella, shredded. Repeat twice more, finishing with noodles, sauce, all remaining mozzarella and the rest of the parmesan.
  7. Cover tightly with foil, tenting it slightly so it doesn't touch the cheese. Bake until hot and bubbling at the edges.
  8. Remove the foil and continue baking until the top is browned in patches and the cheese has set.
  9. Let it sit on the counter. This is not optional, it's what lets the layers firm up enough to cut clean squares instead of collapsing into a puddle.
