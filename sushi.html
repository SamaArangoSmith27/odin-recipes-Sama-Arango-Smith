Sushi

Go back

Seasoned sushi rice and buttery avocado rolled tightly in crisp nori and finished with toasted sesame. Fresh, light and simple, they're a great first step into making sushi at home.

Ingredients:

  • cups sushi rice (short-grain)
  • cups water
  • cups rice vinegar
  • tablespoons sugar
  • teaspoons salt
  • ripe avocados
  • teaspoons lemon juice
  • nori sheets
  • tablespoons toasted sesame seeds
  • tablespoons soy sauce, for serving

Instructions:

  1. Rinse 2 cups sushi rice (short-grain) in a bowl of cold water, swishing and draining, until the water runs mostly clear. This removes surface starch so the rice is sticky but not gummy.
  2. Combine the rice and 2.3 cups water in a pot. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to the lowest heat and cook without lifting the lid.
  3. Take the pot off the heat and leave it covered to finish steaming. Meanwhile, warm 0.3 cups rice vinegar, 2 tablespoons sugar and 1 teaspoons salt together, stirring until dissolved.
  4. Spread the rice in a wide bowl, pour the vinegar mixture over it, and fold gently with a paddle using slicing motions rather than stirring. Fan it as you go and let it cool to room temperature.
  5. Halve, pit and peel 2 ripe avocados, then cut into thin strips. Toss with 1 teaspoons lemon juice to stop browning.
  6. Lay a nori sheet shiny side down on a bamboo mat. With wet hands, spread a thin, even layer of rice over it, leaving a 2 cm strip bare along the far edge. Lay avocado strips in a line across the rice nearest you and sprinkle with 1 tablespoons toasted sesame seeds.
  7. Lift the near edge of the mat and roll it over the filling, tucking tightly. Keep rolling, pressing gently, until you reach the bare strip. Dab it with water to seal. Repeat with the remaining sheets.
  8. Using a very sharp knife wiped with a damp cloth between cuts, slice each roll into 8 pieces. Serve with 3 tablespoons soy sauce, for serving, plus wasabi and pickled ginger if you have them.
